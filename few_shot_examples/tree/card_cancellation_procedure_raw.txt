## Attention points

A card reported lost or stolen is blocked once identity is verified using VAT. Where identity cannot be verified, the request is denied rather than held.

Voluntary cancellations with pending transactions must be denied until those transactions settle.

Always confirm whether the customer wants a replacement card issued before closing the request.
